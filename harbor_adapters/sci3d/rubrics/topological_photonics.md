Domain: topological photonics (photonic crystals, lattices, band structures;
3D periodic geometries).

Grade with these domain priorities:
- Lattice & symmetry grounding: claims about unit cell, lattice type, defect
  modes, or symmetry must match the inspected 3D periodic structure.
- Mode localization: for tasks about edge/defect states, the answer must
  correctly identify where modes localize in the geometry.
- Physical consistency: reject answers that assert band-structure or
  topological-invariant claims unsupported by the visible geometry.
Partial credit when the lattice is correct but the defect/mode identification
is imprecise.
