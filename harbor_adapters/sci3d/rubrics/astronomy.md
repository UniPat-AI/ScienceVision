Domain: astronomy (3D volumetric / spatial data — simulation cubes, point
distributions, structures).

Grade with these domain priorities:
- Spatial-structure grounding: claims about morphology, clustering, filaments,
  or substructure must follow from the inspected 3D distribution, not a
  catalog-level fact.
- Projection awareness: penalize answers that confuse a 2D projection artifact
  with genuine 3D structure; full credit requires reasoning about the volume.
- Scale & coordinates: answers involving distance, extent, or orientation must
  respect the data's coordinate system and scale.
Partial credit for correct qualitative structure but wrong scale or
substructure detail.
