## Medical Audit Evidence

The user-provided local artifact `题型审查-医学外壳几何内核.html`, SHA-256
`74739d6d9bf97246c2315e73365263306e51fdfe5ca737b95bb55bb944bf7e3a`, is
separate audit evidence. It reports a reviewed set of 4 packages / 240 tasks /
321 subquestions whose 11 question types are geometry measurements, with zero
diagnosis, differential, anatomy naming, or clinical-significance fields. It
also reports that a supplied mask overlay makes the medical target available
before the solver reasons about the image, and that an audit reproduced a
sample's values from `mask.nii.gz` without opening CT data.

This does not claim that every future medical asset has the same defect. It
does establish a concrete anti-pattern for the pipeline: a medical question is
not medically eligible if its answer is unchanged when the image is removed
and the supplied segmentation/mask geometry is retained.

## Derived Policy Candidates

The following are derived policies, not additional quotations from the
workbook. Their machine-readable source references live in
`derived/triage_claims.json`.

1. Medical tasks require image-grounded clinical or anatomical reasoning.
   A supplied mask by itself cannot make a geometry calculation a medical
   question.
2. Solver-facing task text must not instruct use of a named tool or expose
   internal procedures and metadata such as `Select overlay`, mask labels,
   bbox bounds, voxel axis/index, FOV midpoint, or viewer implementation
   details. A controlled viewer may provide allowed interactions, but those
   interactions are not the question.
3. Biology, chemistry, and medical tasks fail closed when required evidence is
   not observable in the published viewer. Missing coordinates, centroids,
   bbox/PCA capability, vdW definitions, or stable atom/residue identifiers
   cannot be repaired by author-side computation.
4. Prompt wording, answer mode, definitions, identifier mapping, viewer
   evidence, and truth must pass one contract check. A signed/absolute
   measurement mismatch, redundant framing, or invisible identifier fails the
   candidate.

## Current-thread Direction: Family Mix and GT Authority

The following is a direct user request from this thread on 2026-08-28, not a
Feishu quote: Biology, chemistry, and medical all have measurement saturation
and insufficient property/attribute analysis. Measurement questions should be
a minority; property, attribute, and comparison questions should be primary.
The user also requires that agents cannot create ground truth on their own.

The same user direction adds medical-specific constraints: subdomains are
question types while asset facets remain independent; the M3D-RefSeg doctor
description becomes semantic GT for finding/location/impression at its original
granularity; no solver mask/overlay is allowed; and a hidden mask may only
serve as a reference for a calibrated-tolerance localize-then-measure task.
MSD Task04 hippocampus label 1 (anterior) and label 2 (posterior) must remain
separate. Coverage must deduplicate repeated dataset/facet/question-kernel
combinations so that 35 near-identical items from one dataset do not claim
false diversity. These are current-thread product directions, not Feishu
workbook quotes.

Before generation, each domain coverage plan must publish its family mix and
approved truth provenance. The exact numerical quota remains a profile-owner
decision after asset and evidence inventory; this triage deliberately does not
invent one.

| Domain | Primary candidate families | Measurement role | Required GT provenance types |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Biology | docking-pose plausibility attributes; local interaction/collision properties; multi-structure or state comparisons | Minority supporting evidence, never the whole scientific claim | Expert structural annotation; curated structure record with explicit viewer-ID mapping; approved viewer-native receipt as supporting evidence |
| Chemistry | stereochemical/conformer class; coordination or steric-accessibility property; conformer/site/geometry comparisons | Minority supporting evidence, not a standalone domain claim | Curated molecular stereochemistry/conformer record; expert chemistry annotation; approved viewer-native receipt with a locked definition |
| Medical | anatomical structure/region attribute; imaging feature/morphology property; approved paired-study or region comparisons | Minority and only after image-grounded target establishment | Radiologist annotation/report tied to study; approved clinical/anatomical label tied to visible image evidence; expert review with a locked evidence packet |

An agent may draft language, find an approved evidence item, or format an
already approved truth. It must not fabricate a label, diagnosis, relation,
threshold, or reference answer. A segmentation mask alone is not diagnostic
ground truth. Every truth assertion must enter the candidate contract with a
locked provenance record and the required expert approval.

## Integration Boundary

This source bundle remains non-authoritative: it cannot enable a task or
approve truth. Its typed policy artifacts now live under
`qa_gen_pipeline/configs/sources/feedback/v3_2_expert_feedback/accepted/` and
are eligible to be bound into a *draft* knowledge snapshot for their named
leaf. They deliberately retain `production_approval: false`.

The associated pipeline integration must, before an item can be generated or
released:

- reject solver-facing procedural/tool instructions and metadata shortcuts;
- require a medical clinical-semantic relevance receipt and no-overlay / no-
  metadata shortcut audit;
- make measurement an explicit minority quota in each domain coverage plan;
- require candidate family and GT-provenance declarations before an agent call;
- reject truth records whose provenance is agent-generated rather than locked
  expert, curated, or approved viewer-native evidence.

In particular, the M3D-RefSeg declaration still has `source_status: missing`.
The typed medical policies express allowable source types, not a bound doctor
annotation, so they cannot produce a medical ground truth until an immutable
annotation snapshot, verbatim provenance, and expert approval exist.

No part of this triage or its accepted policy artifacts enables generation or
release. They make the next implementation review evidence-backed and fail
closed.
