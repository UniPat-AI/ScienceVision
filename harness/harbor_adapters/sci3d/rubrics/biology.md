Domain: structural biology (proteins, nucleic acids, molecular complexes;
mmCIF/PDB assets).

Grade with these domain priorities:
- Spatial grounding: the answer must reflect the actual 3D arrangement of
  residues / cofactors / binding sites, not sequence- or annotation-level
  facts that could be recited without opening the structure.
- Occlusion & viewpoint: for viewpoint tasks, full credit requires the chosen
  camera genuinely exposes the named regions with minimal mutual occlusion;
  penalize poses that bury the target behind bulk density.
- Chemical plausibility: reject answers that name binding partners, chains, or
  cofactors not present in the asset.
Partial credit when the region is correct but the orientation/precision is off.
