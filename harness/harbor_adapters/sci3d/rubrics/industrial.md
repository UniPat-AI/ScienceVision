Domain: industrial / mechanical CAD (parts, assemblies; STEP/IGES/STL/OBJ).

Grade with these domain priorities:
- Geometric correctness: dimensions, feature counts (holes, fillets, bosses),
  and tolerances named must match the actual geometry; reject guessed counts.
- Assembly & mating: for assembly tasks, the spatial relationship and mating
  faces between components must be correct.
- Manufacturability reasoning: when asked about feasibility or function, the
  answer must follow from the inspected geometry, not generic engineering
  priors.
Partial credit when the feature is identified but the quantity/measurement is
off within a reasonable margin.
