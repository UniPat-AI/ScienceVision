# Synthetic example

All task IDs, model identifiers, scores, timings, and coverage counts in this directory are invented to demonstrate the format. They are not ScienceVision evaluation results.

The example declares four expected trials: one citable score of 0.5, one blocked score of 0.0, one unscored error, and one missing trial. The native collector therefore reports a citable mean of 0.5 over one trial and a diagnostic all-eligible mean of 0.25 over two trials. Neither is a full-benchmark score.

The dataset/harness references and container digests illustrate real pinned resource references. They do not mean that this synthetic example was produced by those resources.
